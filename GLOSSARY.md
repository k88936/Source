## Asset

A photo or video file in Cloudreve; the smallest unit of the timeline and of albums.

## Album

A Cloudreve directory that the user browses and organizes as an album.

## Folder

Cloudreve's container primitive. In v1 every Album maps to a Folder, but not every Folder is an Album.

## Timeline

A view of Assets across Albums, ordered by capture time descending, backed by the local Catalog.

## Catalog

The app-local derived inventory of known Assets and Albums, rebuildable from Cloudreve at any time.

## Curation

Selections the user attaches to an Album or Asset that the files themselves do not contain, such as cover and favorites.
